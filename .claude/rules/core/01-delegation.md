# Delegation

Use delegation only when the active tool supports it, the task benefits from independent bounded work, and user or harness policy permits it.

- The coordinator owns scope, assumptions, task packets, integration, and final verification.
- A worker owns explicit files or a clearly bounded read-only question.
- Workers must preserve unrelated edits and must not broaden their scope.
- Avoid parallel writers on overlapping files or shared experiment artifacts.
- Reports from another agent are evidence to verify, not a substitute for inspecting the resulting files and commands.
